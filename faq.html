FAQ

Quick answers.

What is Basin Powered Dispatch?

A dispatch board for oilfield haulers. Orders come in, drivers get offers on their phone, and operators see truck status live.

Who is it for?

Trucking companies running about 10 to 75 trucks (vac, water and fluid haulers) and the operators who hire them.

What does it cost?

Free to start. Carriers get their first 30 dispatch jobs free. Operators get their first 10 job orders free. Paid plans scale with fleet size. Ask us on a demo.

Does it handle billing or payments?

No. We stick to dispatch. No money runs through the platform, and your billing stays how it is today.

Can operators and carriers both use it?

Yes. Carriers dispatch their own drivers. Operators can see who's available, pick a carrier, or send a job to all of them.

Where do you work?

We're starting in the Permian Basin and expanding from there.

How do I join?

Tap Try it free, tell us if you're a carrier or an operator, and we'll get you set up. No contract.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The demo is a sample hauling company you can click through as the dispatcher, a driver, or an operator. Nothing is real and nothing gets sent.
