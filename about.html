About

Most dispatching is still done by hand.

The oilfield has modernized almost everything. Getting trucks from a job order to a location still runs on phone calls and whiteboards. We built Basin Powered Dispatch with partners in the Permian Basin to change that.

Our mission

Bring operators and carriers onto one network so trucks get on the road fast, any hour of any day the basin needs them.

Our vision

A growing network where operators and carriers work as true partners, spending less time on the phone and more time growing their business. Built with and for the energy industry, powering dispatch across North America.

What's missing

Plenty of software moves freight. Little of it dispatches.

Good transportation systems are out there. Few are built for the dispatch itself: taking a job order, getting trucks on the road, seeing the job through to a closed ticket and getting those trucks back out.

That gap is revenue leakage. For carriers, it's the 2 AM call that goes unanswered and the job that goes to someone else. For operators, it's the man-hours spent calling around to find trucks.
