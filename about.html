About Basin Powered Dispatch

Most dispatching is still done by hand.

The oilfield has modernized almost everything. But getting a truck from a job order to a location still runs on phone calls, texts and whiteboards.

The industry has made real advances. Still, connecting a dispatcher, a driver and a location is hard to automate. And it's even harder to see who's actually available to take the job.

We heard the same thing from operators and trucking companies alike. The challenge isn't the work. It's getting the right trucks moving, at any hour.

Where the money leaks

Both sides lose when dispatch breaks down.

Carriers

The 2 AM call.

The business runs 24/7. Keeping dispatchers on around the clock is hard. A call missed at 2, 3 or 4 AM is a job that goes to someone else.

$10K–$100K+a year in missed jobs, for some carriers
Operators

Hours on the phone.

For operators the cost is man-hours. Calling company after company to find trucks, then calling back for updates. Time that should go to running the field.

Call after calljust to find out who has trucks open
